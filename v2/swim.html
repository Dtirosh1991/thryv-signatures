Paste it into Outlook

New Outlook or Outlook on the web

  1. Click the gear (Settings), top right.
  2. Go to Account, then Signatures.
  3. Click + New signature and call it Thryv Swim.
  4. Click in the big box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  5. Under Choose default signatures, pick Thryv Swim for new messages and for replies/forwards.
  6. Click Save. Delete your old signature if you like.

Classic Outlook on Windows

  1. Go to File, then Options, then Mail.
  2. Click Signatures...
  3. Click New and call it Thryv Swim.
  4. Click in the edit box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  5. On the right, set Thryv Swim for new messages and replies/forwards.
  6. Click OK twice.

Outlook on your phone

The phone app only does plain text signatures. Open Outlook, tap your profile picture, then the settings gear, then Signature, and type the mailbox details. Emails from your computer will show the full design.
